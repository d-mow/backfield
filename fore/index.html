About
How Fore works
01

Score a hole

Tap the big Strokes box to record par. Use − and + to adjust from there. Putts, fairway and green in regulation are optional — skip them and the round still works.

02

Move between holes

Tap any hole in the strip to jump to it. Save advances to the next hole and rolls from the ninth to the tenth on its own. Nothing is locked — go back and change a hole any time.

03

Read the marks

Scores are marked the way they are on paper.

Birdie
Eagle or better
Bogey
Double or worse

Par is left unmarked.

04

Set up a course

In Settings → Round, tap the par chip on any hole to cycle 3 → 4 → 5. Drag the slider or use ± for distance. Distance is optional. You can edit the course mid-round without losing scores.

Switch between yards and metres in Settings → Preferences. That's a display choice only — distances are always stored and exported in yards.

05

Save and reopen rounds

Save from the bottom of the Tot tab — finished or not. Saved rounds live in Log. Open one and hit Edit to pull it back in as the active round; saving again replaces it rather than making a duplicate.

06

Back up your rounds

Rounds are stored in this browser, not on a server. Clearing your browser data clears them. Settings → Data → Export to Excel writes a real spreadsheet you can keep — and import back later, or on another device.

07

Works offline

Fore needs no account and no connection. Add it to your home screen from Safari's Share menu and it opens like a normal app.
